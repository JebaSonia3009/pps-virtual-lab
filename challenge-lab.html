Challenge problems

Forty problems across the five units. Each one is a situation rather than an instruction: read it, work out the approach yourself, and write it in Write your own before you look at anything here. The solution is deliberately hidden until you ask for it, because the value is in the working out, not in the reading.

A hint is there if you are stuck. When you do reveal a solution, step through it — several of these use a trick worth recognising, and the note underneath says what it is and where else it applies.
